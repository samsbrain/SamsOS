#Purpose
SamOS exists to reduce cognitive load so that I can devote my attention to becoming an exceptional surgeon while living a life I am proud of.
Its purpose is not to maximize productivity.
Its purpose is to make the right decisions easier and more consistent.
It is the external brain that lets Sam spend his mental energy on surgery, relationships, and learning instead of remembering what he's supposed to be doing.


#Mission
1. Build systems instead of relying on memory
2. Protect what matters.
3. Improve continuously.
4. Leave tomorrow easier than today.
5. Most important: My attention is my most valuable and limited resource. SamOS exists to protect it
6. SamOS should remember so I don't have to.

#Core Principles
1. Patient care comes first.
2. Recovery is part of performance.
3. Family is protected.
4. Consistency compounds.
5. Systems beat willpower.
6. Knowledge should never be lost.
7. Every day should move me one step closer to the person I want to become.
#North Star
- Become an exceptional surgeon, husband, athlete, lifelong learner, and steward of my finances without sacrificing my health or my relationships.
- Every day, SamOS should answer one question:
"Given everything I have going on today, what is the next best thing for me to do?"

#Design Philosophy
- Every feature must reduce friction.
- Every rule must have a rationale.
- Every template should be reusable.
- If something requires constant manual maintenance, redesign it.
