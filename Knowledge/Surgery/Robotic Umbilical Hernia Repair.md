# Robotic Umbilical Hernia Repair with Mesh

## Case pattern

- Robotic umbilical hernia repair with coated mesh
- Approximately 2 cm fascial defect
- Large amount of omental fat within the hernia defect

## Operative steps and pearls

1. **Gain abdominal access.** Use Veress entry at Palmer's point.
2. **Perform diagnostic laparoscopy.** Confirm that there is no bowel within the
   hernia before proceeding.
3. **Place the robotic ports.** Begin with the camera port and confirm that the
   planned port sites will not impinge against the ASIS or rib cage. Triangulate
   the operative field and leave sufficient working room to develop the
   peritoneal flap.
4. **Reduce the hernia contents and size the mesh.** Plan approximately 4-5 cm
   of mesh overlap beyond each edge of the defect, with another 1-2 cm to
   accommodate the peritoneal flap.
5. **Complete the repair.** In this case, a 2 x 2 cm defect was repaired with a
   9 x 9 cm coated mesh and a peritoneal flap.

## Robotic suturing pearls

1. When tying, move the right hand holding the short end of the suture toward
   the point where the knot should seat. This conserves suture and avoids
   pulling through more length than necessary. On the first throw, the left
   hand should be the only hand moving.
2. STRATAFIX can be used to fixate the mesh. Pass the needle centrally through
   the mesh, take half of the next bite, and leave the needle in place.
